A view of the planned workflow

01 · PLAYERS

Registration & review

Players submit their details; league organizers review registrations before players enter the tournament pool.

02 · TEAMS

Teams & owners

Organizers prepare franchises, assign owners and define the purse and squad rules for the season.

03 · AUCTION

Player auction

Eligible players can be presented as lots, with bids and sale decisions intended to update team squads.

04 · MATCH DAY

Scorekeeper console

An assigned official records the match, while league operations control scoring access and match status.

05 · LIVE

Scoreboard & standings

Fans can follow match progress, results, team standings and player statistics from public-facing views.

06 · OPERATIONS

Admin workspace

Authorized administrators are intended to oversee approvals, fixtures, auction operations and match permissions.
